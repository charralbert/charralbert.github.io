The Inspiration and Further Application

I created this architecture with the idea to create four total identity profiles: Affiliative, Aggressive, Passive, and Avoidant (the identity I created). These draw from identity profiles seen in goats [1]. In brief: Affiliative would transition from naturally-easy going to happy, Aggressive would transition from hostile to respectful, and Passive would transition from careless to content. The summer is quite a short period of time and thus, I wasn't able to create all the identities. However, I would be interested to implement all personalities and conduct a study to determine which identity profile is most rewarding for users. I am curious of things like: Does an initially hostile robot ruin the interaction forever; or does it make an interaction with a non-human creature more realistic and thus rewarding when trusted?
